### Slide 9 – Auto in Cowork adjusts reasoning effort and runs subagents

**Auto in Cowork now sets the reasoning effort for each step and splits complex work across subagents.**

- **Adaptive Effort** — Quick lookups stay fast; complex work gets deeper reasoning.
- **Subagents in Parallel** — Each part uses the best-suited model, then results combine into one output.
- **Parallel Research** — A competitive analysis researches every competitor at once, into one brief.
- **Frontier Rollout** — Available now in Frontier, powered by Microsoft-Decision-1.1.

**Source:** [What's new in Copilot Cowork at Ignite 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/whats-new-in-copilot-cowork-at-ignite-2026/4572242) · [Home: Your starting place for work in the Microsoft Copilot app](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/home-your-starting-place-for-work-in-the-microsoft-copilot-app/4573945) · [Microsoft Ignite 2026 Book of News](https://news.microsoft.com/events/ignite/2026/book-of-news/)  
**IDs:** none listed

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Auto is the smart router for the new Copilot, choosing the models and effort for each task to balance quality, speed, and cost. Cowork is an agentic platform that plans and finishes multistep work using several leading models and picking the right one for each task. Auto now adjusts reasoning effort for each task: quick lookups stay fast and complex work gets deeper reasoning. Behind the scenes, Auto uses Microsoft-Decision-1.1 to make that call on every step, and users can still set the effort level themselves. For more complex tasks, Auto splits the work across multiple models with subagents that run side by side, each using the model best suited to its part, then brings everything back together into one finished result. Also covered in Home: Your starting place for work in the Microsoft Copilot app; Microsoft Ignite 2026 Book of News.

Auto with effort levels and subagents is rolling out to Frontier customers.

</details>
