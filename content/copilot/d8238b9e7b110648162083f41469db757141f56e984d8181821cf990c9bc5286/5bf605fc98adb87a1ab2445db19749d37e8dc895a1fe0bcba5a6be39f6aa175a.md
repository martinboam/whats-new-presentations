## User capabilities

### Slide 5 – Home brings Chat and Cowork together with projects

**Home in the Copilot app brings Chat and Cowork into one starting point, with projects that keep related work together.**

- **Home and Navigation** — Chat and Cowork start in one place, with a shared pane for Plugins, Automations, and Workspaces.
- **Combined History** — One Chat and Cowork history, with search and filters, helps users return to the right thread.
- **Projects** — Related chats, tasks, instructions, files, and outputs stay together, so users don’t rebuild context.
- **Rollout Timing** — Home: Frontier in October, worldwide in November; projects reach Copilot Chat in November.

**Source:** [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528)  
**IDs:** Projects: Message center MC1519856

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Home is now the starting point for work in the Copilot app, bringing together Chat and Cowork in one place with a new shared navigation pane. Chat answers questions, explores ideas, takes actions, and creates first drafts, while Cowork takes on complex, multi-step tasks; the pane brings together Plugins, Automations, Workspaces, and Projects. A combined Chat and Cowork conversation history with search and filters helps users quickly return to the right thread. Projects give users a dedicated space for related chats, tasks, instructions, and context, with project-wide instructions, shared files, meetings, and chats, and a centralized Outputs view for files that project tasks create.

Home and the shared navigation pane are rolling out to Frontier in October and worldwide in November. 

Projects rolled out in Cowork in October and are rolling out in Copilot Chat in November.

</details>

**Skills now come to Copilot Chat, turning know-how into reusable capabilities that Copilot applies automatically.**

- **Create in Chat** — Users create their own skills directly in Copilot Chat using natural language.
- **Discover and Share** — Skills shared by colleagues or the organization are found in the Plugins section.
- **Consistent Practices** — Users choose when skills apply, helping teams scale consistent practices across Copilot.
- **November Rollout** — Rolls out in November.

**Source:** [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528)  
**IDs:** Message center MC1493223

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Skills help turn know-how into reusable capabilities that Copilot can apply automatically, enabling faster execution, more consistent results, and easier knowledge sharing across teams. Skills are expanding from Cowork to Copilot Chat, bringing reusable expertise directly into the flow of everyday work. Users can create their own skills in Copilot Chat using natural language, discover skills shared by colleagues or their organization through the Plugins section, and choose when and how skills are used in their workflows. By capturing reusable instructions and expertise, skills help teams scale consistent practices across Copilot experiences.

</details>

### Slide 7 – Office in the Copilot app and a refreshed Copilot design in Office

**Copilot brings the full power of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint into the Copilot app, while Copilot in the Office apps adopts the Copilot app design.**

- **Office in Copilot** — Files open side by side with the conversation, with full editing, sharing, comments, and coauthoring.
- **Library** — Recent Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files, pages, and images in one place, stored in OneDrive.
- **Refreshed Design** — Copilot in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint gets a simpler chat and response layout that matches the Copilot app.
- **Rollout Timing** — Office in Copilot is rolling out to Frontier in Chat and Cowork; the refreshed design rolled out in October.

**Source:** [Introducing Office in the new Microsoft Copilot app](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/introducing-office-in-the-new-microsoft-copilot-app/4561230) · [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528)  
**IDs:** Office in Copilot: Message center MC1530611

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Office in Copilot brings the creation and collaboration capabilities of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint into the Copilot app. Users can start in Chat or Cowork, have Copilot create a document that opens side by side with the conversation, keep iterating with follow-up prompts, and make direct edits in the canvas, with sharing, comments, coauthoring, and presence in real time. Library brings recent Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files, pages, and AI-generated images together, and because every file is stored in OneDrive, files keep their formatting, comments, permissions, version history, and security labels. Separately, Copilot in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint is getting a refreshed design that aligns with the Copilot app, simplifying the chat and response layout and improving navigation.

Office in Copilot is rolling out for Frontier customers in Chat and Cowork. The refreshed Copilot design in Word, Excel, and PowerPoint rolled out in October.

</details>

### Slide 9 – Role-based plugins and new connectors for Cowork

**Cowork now starts specialized work from pre-built role workflows and connects to more of the systems teams use every day.**

- **Role-Based Plugins** — Sales, Service, Education, Legal, and Finance plugins package common business processes as skills.
- **Role Skills** — Skills cover work such as meeting preparation, case triage, accreditation evidence, and contract review.
- **New Connectors** — Power Platform and federated Copilot connectors bring business apps and live external data into Cowork.
- **October Rollout** — Role-based plugins rolled out in October; Power Platform connectors roll out over the coming weeks.

**Source:** [What’s New in Cowork: Expanded plugins and role-based skills](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-cowork-expanded-plugins-and-role-based-skills/4566221) · [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528)  
**IDs:** none listed

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Role-based plugins in Cowork provide pre-built skills for common business processes across Sales, Service, Education, Legal, and Finance. They sit in the new Roles & Industry section, so Cowork starts with a predefined workflow instead of a blank prompt. Examples include post-meeting follow-up and pipeline prioritization for Sales, case triage and knowledge harvest for Service, accreditation evidence and accessible learning materials for Education, and contract review and case chronology for Legal; Dynamics 365 users can also add the Dynamics 365 Sales and Customer Service plugins. Cowork also draws on more connectors: Power Platform connectors for the business apps organizations already connect, and federated Copilot connectors that bring live data from external systems through Model Context Protocol using the user’s own permissions. Admins decide which plugins and connectors are available.

Role-based plugins rolled out in October. Power Platform connectors are rolling out over the coming weeks.

</details>

### Slide 10 – OneNote, Planner, and event-driven tasks in Cowork

**Cowork now acts on notes and plans and starts work when the business systems teams use every day change.**

- **OneNote and Planner** — Cowork reads and updates notebooks and manages plans, buckets, goals, and tasks, with user approval.
- **Event-Driven Tasks** — Through plugins and Power Platform connectors, Cowork starts work when a case opens or a deal moves stages.
- **Approval Built In** — Tasks run with the user’s permissions and wait for approval before sending a message or changing a record.
- **Rollout Timing** — OneNote and Planner reached Frontier in October and roll out worldwide in November.

**Source:** [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528) · [What’s New in Cowork: Expanded plugins and role-based skills](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-cowork-expanded-plugins-and-role-based-skills/4566221)  
**IDs:** OneNote and Planner: Roadmap [567315](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=567315)

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

OneNote and Planner capabilities bring users’ notes, decisions, tasks, and plans into the context Cowork works from: Cowork can read and update notebooks with the OneNote plugin, and view, create, and update plans, buckets, goals, and tasks in natural language, writing progress back with the user’s approval. Event-driven tasks now go beyond email and Teams messages: through plugins and Power Platform connectors, Cowork can watch for a change in business systems, such as a new case or a deal that moves stages, and start the work. Users describe the trigger in plain language, review a card that shows the event, the action, and the permissions needed, and manage it from the Automations page. Tasks run with the user’s permissions, see only what the user can see, and wait for approval before sending a message or changing a record.

OneNote and Planner capabilities rolled out to Frontier in October and are rolling out worldwide in November. Supported event triggers are defined by partners in each plugin or connector.

</details>

### Slide 13 – Copilot Workspaces brings teams, files, and Office editing together

**Copilot Workspaces, the evolution of Copilot Notebooks, gives teams a shared space in the Copilot app to work alongside Copilot on an ongoing workstream.**

- **Team Workspaces** — Context, team members, and working files come together, with live cursors across documents.
- **Office File Editing** — Users ask Copilot to create or edit Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files and refine them in place.
- **Audio Overview Updates** — Topic-based episodes, inherited sensitivity labels, more reference types, and new formats.
- **Rollout Timing** — Rolled out to Frontier in October; Audio Overview goes worldwide in November and Office editing in January.

**Source:** [What’s New in Microsoft Copilot | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-in-microsoft-copilot--october-2026/4568528) · [What's New in Notebooks | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/whats-new-in-notebooks--october-2026/4559105)  
**IDs:** Audio Overview Updates: Roadmap [570966](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=570966) · More reference file types in Workspaces: Roadmap [569211](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=569211), [569210](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=569210)

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Copilot Workspaces is the evolution of Copilot Notebooks: it brings context, team members, and working files together so teams can create, refine, and advance an ongoing workstream, and existing notebooks will become workspaces. Teams can collaborate across different documents at the same time and see what others are working on with live cursors. Users can ask Copilot in chat to create or edit Word, Excel, and PowerPoint files, then work directly in those files with Office capabilities without leaving the workspace. Audio Overview adds topic-based episodes, sensitivity labels inherited from sources when saved to OneDrive, more reference types such as Outlook emails, Teams meetings and chats, OneNote and Loop pages, and .txt files, and new formats such as Critique and Debate. Workspaces also accept more reference types, including JPG, PNG, CSV, and TSV files, and Markdown, TXT, and RTF files, so images, lists, and tables can ground Copilot alongside documents. Also covered in What's New in Notebooks | October 2026.

Copilot Workspaces rolled out to Frontier in October. Office file editing rolled out to Frontier in October and rolls out worldwide in January. Audio Overview updates rolled out to Frontier in October and roll out worldwide in November. JPG, PNG, CSV, and TSV references rolled out in October, and Markdown, TXT, and RTF references are generally available.

</details>

### Slide 14 – Copilot creates notebooks, does simple work, and runs automations

**Copilot Notebooks gains agentic help, from building a notebook from a goal to doing simple work and running recurring tasks.**

- **Notebook Creation** — Users describe a goal and Copilot builds a notebook with relevant references from work and the web.
- **Simple Work** — Copilot manages references, synthesizes project context, and creates or edits single Office files from chat.
- **Simple Automations** — Recurring tasks, like a daily brief or weekly status deck, run on a schedule from one prompt.
- **Frontier Rollout** — Simple work is available in Frontier; notebook creation and automations arrive in the coming weeks.

**Source:** [What's New in Notebooks | October 2026](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft-copilot-blog/whats-new-in-notebooks--october-2026/4559105)  
**IDs:** Suggested artifacts in Notebooks: Roadmap [566870](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap?id=566870); Message center MC1411727

<details><summary>Speaker notes</summary>

Copilot Notebooks is gaining more ways to move work forward. Instead of setting up a notebook manually, users describe their goal and Copilot puts together relevant references across their work data and the web, and a refreshed experience in the Copilot app and OneNote lets users group and reorder content and artifacts. Copilot can now do simple work: manage references to keep notebooks current, synthesize information across project context, and create and edit single Word, Excel, or PowerPoint files from chat, with prompts scoped to the notebook or to Work and web. Simple automations schedule recurring tasks, such as a daily morning brief or weekly status deck, with templates, frequency settings, and monitoring. Notebooks also add interactive reports, PDF creation through chat, and suggested artifacts that recommend Word, Excel, or PowerPoint files based on Work IQ and notebook content.

Delegating simple work, model choice, the new UX, interactive reports, and PDF creation are available in Frontier. Notebook creation with Copilot and simple automations are coming in the next few weeks. Suggested artifacts are generally available.

</details>

Enhancements:
- Expanded connector support — Use Power Platform and Copilot connectors to connect Cowork to more external data sources and services.
- Non-Microsoft event-driven triggers — Start tasks from events in supported non-Microsoft connectors and plugins, such as when a new case is added in a customer relationship management system.

### What’s new in Copilot Studio (slides 49–50)

The published slide labels these entries **September 2026**. They are retained here as historical reference, not relabelled as October announcements:

- **Extract node:** workflows can extract named values and tables from documents such as invoices, contracts and financial statements for use in later steps.
- **Copilot node (preview):** workflows can run Copilot Chat or a Cowork task using the connected user's work context, and call supported agents.
- **Model selection:** a dropdown selects a model for agents powered by the GitHub Copilot harness, with experimental models available for evaluation.
- **Content safety testing:** configurable severity thresholds help evaluate responses for potentially harmful content.
- **File attachments (general availability as labelled on the slide):** support expands to Excel, PowerPoint and Word files.
- **Dataverse knowledge:** Dataverse tables ground agent answers; the slide separately labels unstructured reasoning over Multiline Text and File columns as preview. Do not treat that preview as a statement that every Dataverse capability is generally available.
